Honest caveats

This has not been validated prospectively. The window [arr−22, arr−8] was chosen by scanning 8,316 features over these same 26 journeys. The permutation test corrects for the size of that search (p=0.0002), and the result survives a chronological split and holds across neighbouring windows — but the real test is whether it keeps working on journeys recorded after 2026-08-26. Treat the next several trips as the trial.

n is small. 26 journeys, 12 of them right-hand. One or two surprising trips could move these numbers noticeably.

The final verdict needs you to press Stop. It reads the window ending 8 seconds before the recording ends, so it depends on your habit of stopping within about a second of the train halting. The live trace does not depend on this, which is why the two differ.

Route-specific. The arming time and braking thresholds are fitted to Stockwell→Brixton and this boarding habit. Nothing checks at runtime that you are actually on that journey.
